3. Win+R -> powershell -> Enter. Asagidaki iki komutu calistirin:

cd "C:\Users\tarii\ikurt-projects\ikurt-izle"
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\streaming\Start-Local.ps1

5. Tarayicida http://localhost:5173/c/local-synthetic acin. Yalniz renkli test goruntusu olacak.

node streaming/smoke-local.mjs

powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\streaming\Stop-Local.ps1
